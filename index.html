The Destination

Lokobe National Park — Madagascar's Last Rainforest on Nosy Be

Lokobe is the last remaining tract of primary forest on Nosy Be. This protected reserve is a living treasure chest of endemic wildlife — species that exist nowhere else in the world. Reaching it by pirogue (traditional outrigger canoe) across turquoise waters is just the beginning of the adventure.

Brookesia Chameleon

Brookesia minima

One of the smallest reptiles on Earth. Barely the size of your thumbnail, these tiny chameleons are masters of disguise on the forest floor.

Black Lemur

Eulemur macaco

The iconic black lemurs of Nosy Be swing through the canopy in family groups. Males are jet black while females are golden-brown — a stunning contrast.

Leaf-tailed Gecko

Uroplatus henkeli

Masters of camouflage, these incredible geckos blend perfectly with bark and branches. Their large eyes and flattened tails make them one of Lokobe's most fascinating finds.

Mouse Lemur

Microcebus sp.

The world's smallest primates, these adorable nocturnal creatures hide in tree hollows during the day and emerge at dusk with their enormous curious eyes.

Madagascar Scops Owl

Otus rutilus

A rare nocturnal sighting for those who join the evening tours. Their haunting call echoes through the ancient trees at twilight.

Leaf-nosed Snake

Langaha madagascariensis

With its extraordinary leaf-shaped snout, this harmless snake is one of Lokobe's most unusual residents — perfectly adapted to blend with the forest vines.

Turtle Island · Nosy Be

Nosy Iranja

⏱️ Duration ~8 hours
💰 From €60 / person
🚤 Boat ride ~2h each way

Nosy Iranja — Madagascar's most iconic island escape. Two islets connected by a stunning 1.2 km white sandbar at low tide, turquoise waters teeming with sea turtles, and a small fishing village where time moves slowly. This is the day trip everyone who visits Nosy Be puts first on their list.

What's Included

  • Boat transfer (return)
  • Malagasy beach lunch
  • Snorkeling gear
  • Park entrance fee
  • Guide
  • Bottled water

Highlights

🏖️
The Sandbar

At low tide, a 1.2 km white sandbar appears and connects the two islets — one of the most photographed spots in Madagascar.

🐢
Sea Turtles

Swim and snorkel alongside sea turtles in crystal-clear water with visibility up to 20 metres. This is their nesting island.

🏠
Fishing Village

A traditional Malagasy fishing village on the main island — meet local families, taste fresh seafood, and see authentic island life.

🗼
Historic Lighthouse

Climb to the lighthouse (reportedly designed by Gustave Eiffel) for a breathtaking panoramic view of both islets and the open ocean.

Lemur Island · Nosy Be

Nosy Komba

⏱️ Duration ~7 hours
💰 From €40 / person
🚤 Boat ride ~30 min each way

Nosy Komba — Madagascar's legendary Lemur Island. A volcanic island with no cars, no crowds, and lush jungle trails home to the iconic black lemur. From the summit, panoramic views stretch over Nosy Be and the turquoise sea. Wander through the craft village of Ampangorina and bring home hand-embroidered linens and carved wooden masks.

What's Included

  • Boat transfer (return)
  • Malagasy lunch
  • Village guide
  • Bottled water

Highlights

🦍
Black Lemurs

Endemic to Nosy Komba and Nosy Be. Males are jet black with amber eyes; females are reddish-brown — you'll encounter them up close on the forest trails.

🌿
Jungle Trek

Steep forest paths wind through vanilla, cacao, and coffee plants. Spot chameleons, exotic birds, and giant jackfruit along the way.

🏔️
Panoramic Summit

Reach the top of the island for sweeping 360° views over Nosy Komba's peaks, Nosy Be, and the endless Indian Ocean.

🛍️
Craft Village

Stroll through Ampangorina's vibrant market — hand-embroidered tablecloths, carved wooden masks, lambas, and local art. Craftsmanship passed down for generations.

Natural Aquarium · Nosy Be

Nosy Tanikely

⏱️ Duration ~6 hours
💰 From €45 / person
🚤 Boat ride ~35 min each way

Nosy Tanikely — Madagascar's finest natural aquarium. A tiny protected island 10 km from Nosy Be, surrounded by the healthiest coral reef in the region. Slip on your snorkel mask and within minutes you're swimming alongside green and hawksbill turtles, with visibility up to 30 metres. No diving experience needed — this is snorkeling for everyone.

What's Included

  • Boat transfer (return)
  • Beach picnic lunch
  • Snorkeling gear
  • Park entrance fee
  • Guide
  • Bottled water

Highlights

🐢
Sea Turtles

Green and hawksbill turtles patrol the shallow seagrass beds just metres from shore. Spotting them is almost guaranteed.

🐠
Natural Aquarium

The best snorkeling in Madagascar — coral gardens start just 20–30 m from the beach, with clownfish, parrotfish, barracuda, and moorish idol in crystal-clear water.

🗼
Lighthouse Hike

A short trail leads to the hilltop lighthouse with sweeping panoramic views over Nosy Be, Nosy Komba, and the endless blue horizon.

🦎
Wild Lemurs

Brown lemurs roam freely through the island's tropical forest. Along with flying foxes, they share this uninhabited paradise with you.
